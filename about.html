Styling and creative direction, done with intention

Fainica Consultancy is a creative consultancy founded by Faith Malesoena and Duduzile Monica Mncube. Built on the principle of faithful guidance and intentional design, we help individuals and brands bring their visions to life through curated spaces, styling, and creative direction.

Our work focuses on creating beautiful, functional, and meaningful experiences through interior styling, content creation and event co-ordination.

About us

Our team

Duduzile Monica Mncube

Founder

A creative with an eye for detail and a deep appreciation for beauty in everyday elements, colour, texture, light, and space.

With a background in Fine & Applied Arts, Duduzile evolved into a visual artist before expanding into interior decorating, styling, and wardrobe consultancy. She brings a playful yet refined artistic touch, transforming ordinary spaces and concepts into visually striking experiences.

If it can be styled, she is already reimagining it.

Faith Malesoena

Co-founder

A creative at heart, structured, practical, and intentional. Faith has the ability to imagine bold ideas and strategically bring them to life. With a strong background in administration, she is highly organised, observant, and always thinking ahead.

She has a natural eye for detail and effortless style, from personal presentation to transforming spaces. She brings balance to the brand: creativity grounded in logic.

The calm thinker. The style curator. The one who turns ideas into beautifully executed realities.
